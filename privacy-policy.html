eBuddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 February 2025

eBuddy is a local browser extension for authorised users of the supported court-management website. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any court, government department, or third-party service unless separately stated by the deploying organisation.

Information eBuddy handles

When you use eBuddy, it may read information displayed in the supported website, including names, dates of birth, identifiers, charges, hearings, outcomes, bail or custody information, sentencing information, and related case details. It may also handle local CSV or JSON files that you explicitly select, document templates, email drafts, recipient addresses, and generated documents.

eBuddy does not bundle or preinstall document templates. Templates and email drafts are added by the user or importing organisation and remain local unless deliberately exported or backed up.

How information is used

The information is used only to provide the extension's requested analysis, comparison, document generation, email-draft generation, and local export features. eBuddy does not sell, advertise against, profile, or use case information for unrelated purposes.

Where information goes

eBuddy processes case information locally in the browser. The extension does not send case information, selected CSV/JSON contents, templates, or generated documents to an eBuddy server, analytics provider, advertising network, or other external service.

Information can leave the extension when you deliberately export, download, copy, back up, or save it to a folder. Those files are controlled by your browser and operating system.

Local storage and retention

  • Active profile details are held in the current tab's memory while the workspace is in use.
  • Recently accessed identity entries are stored locally for up to 24 hours after last access.
  • Selected CSV/JSON source-file handles and temporary local copies are stored in the extension's IndexedDB storage while the relevant feature is configured. Disabling the feature or using “Clear cached case data” removes the extension copy and permission; it does not delete the original source file.
  • Templates and document-job records remain locally until you delete them or clear them in eBuddy.
  • A selected export-folder capability remains locally until you clear it or browser permission is revoked.

Browser extension storage is protected by the browser and operating-system user account. eBuddy does not provide encryption at rest. Do not use eBuddy on an account shared with unauthorised people.

Deletion

Use the available clear controls in eBuddy to remove recent identities, extension copies of selected source files, cached case data, completed job records, templates, and saved folder permissions. Clearing extension data never deletes the original CSV or JSON file. Browser profile removal or uninstalling the extension may also remove extension storage, subject to browser behaviour and backups.

Permissions

eBuddy requests only the permissions needed for local browser storage, local file selection and export, the side panel, document generation, and the supported website. It does not use remote code, third-party analytics, or external messaging.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when eBuddy's data handling changes. For privacy questions or deletion support, contact the developer through the eBuddy GitHub issue tracker.
